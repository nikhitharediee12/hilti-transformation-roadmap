Design decisions

The health signal sits top right, in amber, with one sentence. A leadership team reads the verdict first and the reason second; the sentence names the two metrics behind the amber so nobody has to hunt for them.

Phases run left to right in roadmap order. The board mirrors the roadmap page, so a reader can trace any metric back to its initiative by position as well as by name. The Walk column is widest because Walk is where this quarter's evidence is.

Bullet bars instead of gauges or trend lines. Each metric answers one question, how far from target, and a bar with a target tick shows that in the least space. Gauges waste area; trend lines need history Hilti has not published.

Status uses shape as well as colour. A tick, a triangle and a cross carry the same meaning as green, amber and red, so the board still reads in greyscale print or for colour-blind readers.

Decisions sit at the bottom, numbered as in the RACI. The review ends on what leaders must decide before the next QBR, and each item names its single Accountable role.

The "Illustrative" label is on every tile that needs it. Only M1 is a published figure (S4). Labelling the rest keeps the dashboard honest about what Hilti discloses.
