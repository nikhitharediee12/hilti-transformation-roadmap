Who this is for

The newly appointed Head of Digital Business at a mid-sized European construction-products manufacturer. The company sells through its own field sales force, runs one SAP ERP across its countries, and its executive board has asked for a multi-year plan to launch software subscriptions alongside its hardware.

What they can do with it

  • Roadmap
    Use the execution horizon to see which foundation must be live before each Walk initiative, and which of those dependencies are hard prerequisites versus standardization choices.
  • RACI

  • Dashboard

What we added

In Week 4 our team designed an AI feature for Hilti's firestop engineers, and that work kept returning to one lesson: nothing useful runs on data nobody governs. That is why the roadmap places the S/4HANA Global Digital Core in Crawl, even though it went live years before Hilti's software push: it gives later software a standard way to be billed and integrated. We were careful not to overstate that link, so the dependency table marks it as enabling rather than hard. Because Hilti built its foundations as separate waves between 2015 and 2021, the roadmap shows two timelines: the verified history, and an execution horizon in which Crawl fits in four quarters and Run continues from quarter 8 onward. In the RACI we gave architecture exceptions to the Group CIO because Hilti's CIO reports only one modification to SAP standard code, and 4PS, built on Microsoft Dynamics 365, will test that rule. On the dashboard, the first metric we would watch is the share of account managers selling software, because Hilti's direct sales force is its strategy.
