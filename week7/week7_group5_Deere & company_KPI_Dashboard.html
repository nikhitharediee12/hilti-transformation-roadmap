What is the transformation thesis?

Transformation thesis: Deere is shifting from a cyclical equipment maker toward a technology-enabled “Smart Industrial” model, using connected machines, precision technology and software to raise customer value and make earnings more resilient; the dashboard tests whether that shows up in adoption, investment and margins.

Data note: Deere is publicly traded (NYSE: DE), so financial KPIs come from its 10-K and 8-K filings. Adoption KPIs come from Deere’s 2024 Sustainability Disclosures (FY2021–FY2024) and its 2025 Business Impact Report (FY2025), so every year is disclosed and nothing is interpolated. R&D intensity and FY2024 operating margin are calculated from disclosed figures. All other dashboard values are reported figures or rounded milestone disclosures. Calculated values are marked *.

Tip: press play under the charts to step through the years.

  • 2022Leap Ambitions introduced (500M engaged-acre and 1.5M connected-machine targets for 2026). Net sales +20.6% to $47.9B and R&D +20.5% to $1.9B; margin about flat at 17.4%. 329M engaged acres (68M highly engaged) and 500K connected machines.
  • 2023Peak year: net sales $55.6B, margin 21.9%, net income $10.2B, cost of sales ratio 67.9%, driven by price realization and strong volumes. R&D +13.9% to $2.2B. Engaged acres +18% to 388M, highly engaged acres +35% to 92M, 650K connected machines.
  • 2024Downturn begins: net sales -19.4% to $44.8B, margin 18.2%*, but R&D still rose 5.2% to $2.3B (5.1% of sales). 455M engaged acres (125M highly engaged) and 775K connected machines.
  • 2025Margin compresses to 12.6% (tariffs cost about $600M, roughly 1.5 pts) and net income falls to $5.0B, while R&D reaches $2.3B (5.9% of sales). Nearly 500M engaged acres (147M highly engaged, roughly 30%) and about 1M connected machines.

Calculated metrics (*): R&D intensity and FY2024 operating margin are calculated from disclosed figures. All other dashboard values are reported figures or rounded milestone disclosures. R&D intensity = R&D expense / equipment operations net sales. Operating margin = equipment operations operating profit / net sales; FY2021 ($6,868M), FY2022, FY2023 and FY2025 are disclosed, FY2024 is the sum of the three segments’ disclosed operating profit ($8,150M) divided by net sales. Cost of sales ratio = cost of sales / net sales, as stated in Deere’s filings. Engaged acres = unique acres with at least one operation pass documented in the Operations Center in the past 12 months. Highly engaged acres = engaged acres with documentation of multiple production steps and use of digital tools for multiple value-creating activities over 12 months. “Nearly 500M” and “1M” are Deere’s rounded FY2025 disclosures, so FY2025 change figures for the adoption KPIs are approximate. Absolute R&D spend ($1,587M in FY2021 to $2,311M in FY2025) appears in the annotations rather than as a separate KPI. Fiscal years end in late October or early November.
